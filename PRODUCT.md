# Accounts

Accounts is the shared sign-in and identity hub for Aiden Jang's apps.
People sign in with an email and password, Google, or a passkey, then return to their app.
The account hub provides app links, profile editing, sign-in methods, active devices,
account export and account deletion.

The browser remembers recent email addresses only on that device. Remembering an address
does not authenticate it. Returning destinations are validated by the existing client and server rules.
Recovery, verification and sensitive account changes retain their existing identity checks.

The approved visual direction is a light account home with cool ivory, white forms,
slate text, blue actions and the existing key mark. App identity uses bundled icons and
verified destination labels. The desktop and phone mocks approved in this chat define the layout.

Success means one clear next action on sign-in, less account-page scrolling, accessible
forms and navigation, and no loss of entered words or existing authentication behavior.
