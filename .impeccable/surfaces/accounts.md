# Accounts redesign

Visitor mode: Operate. People arrive from an app to sign in or return to manage their identity.
The user approved the desktop and phone comparisons in `auth-before-after.html` in this chat.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A focused account home makes the next task obvious. Sign-in starts with the destination;
returning users choose a remembered account before choosing a sign-in method.

OWN-WORLD: Cool ivory surrounds white forms, slate text and restrained blue actions. DM Sans,
the existing key mark and bundled app icons connect the shared account to its destinations.

STORY: Choose an account or enter an email, authenticate, then return to the validated destination.
The account hub separates apps, profile, sign-in, devices and data while retaining local drafts.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A thin white wordmark header precedes a centered sign-in column. The account
hub has a compact identity row and section navigation, beside content on desktop and above it on phones.

FORM: User-pinned light Accounts design from the approved comparison. seed: none (user-pinned mock; no concept roll).
Native forms, existing recovery paths, passkeys, Google, challenge handling and confirmation rules remain.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict,
DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
